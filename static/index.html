    Place bonusesBonus weight and weekly points

    Assumes places are unvisited and up to 100 weekly points remain. Mark previously collected places on the map or in results, then search again. Scoring requires a successful mesh report. Park eligibility is estimated near mapped boundaries; summits count only the peak square because elevation-qualified areas are unavailable.

    Search strategyBeam, greedy, or comparison
    Beam keeps up to 3 standard routes, then explores wider loops for up to 3 wild alternatives. Wild routes may head away from the destination first, but must respect your time budget, area and waypoint order. Up to 72 refinement requests plus 12 wild-loop checks; greedy up to 12. Fewer than 6 routes may qualify. Compare shares initial routes and reuses matching requests.

    How to use the planner

    0 shows all; 1 is Today, 2 is Yesterday + today, and 3–30 include today and the previous N−1 calendar dates. Uses midnight in your device timezone, not rolling 24-hour periods, for every team. Uses the recorded capture time in your snapshot. Display filter only; route scoring still uses the full board.

    Select the Start field or its Map button, then tap the map to set Start and Finish in order. Further map taps leave both stops unchanged. Select Finish or its Map button to change only Finish. For an address, select Find address and choose the matching location. Include city and state for better results.

    Targets follow the selected mission: Fortify shows friendly reinforcement targets in amber; other modes show capture and exploration targets in green. Targets can include unpainted cells inferred from the observed grid. Dashed green outlines show unpainted exploration with unknown coverage; solid green outlines show enemy targets. Routing prioritizes enemy squares at or below 1 point and sharply discounts higher scores. Missing scores receive reduced priority. Unpainted targets are exploration opportunities. Weighted value is a ranking aid, not a capture probability. Counts are not guaranteed captures. Radio coverage, score, protection, and sampling intervals can prevent capture. Planning directions only; no live GPS guidance or traffic.

    Address searches are sent to Photon (OpenStreetMap data); coordinates skip address lookup. Routing sends trip coordinates to the configured OSRM provider. Map tiles load from OpenStreetMap. Capture data refreshes automatically from MeshWars, with a local fallback cache.
